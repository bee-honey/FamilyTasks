## Description

Family Tasks helps households plan the work of everyday life together.

Sort tasks by urgency and importance, see what is due today, assign work to family members, keep shopping lists by store, and track recurring responsibilities like bills, services, and household chores.

Features:
- Today view for scheduled family tasks
- Task Matrix with Do, Schedule, Delegate, and Drop sections
- Shared shopping lists grouped by shop
- Share a shop list as bullet points through Messages or other apps
- Recurring tasks with daily, weekly, monthly, and yearly schedules
- Profile and family member settings
- Calendar sync for task due dates, including Google calendars configured on the device
- iCloud/CloudKit foundation for family data sharing
