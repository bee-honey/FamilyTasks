# Privacy Policy

Family Tasks does not collect personal data.

Information you create in the app, such as tasks, grocery lists, meal plans, recurring reminders, profile initials, family member entries, and settings, is stored on your device and may sync through your iCloud account if iCloud sharing is enabled.

The developer does not operate a server and does not access, sell, rent, or share your app data.

Calendar access is optional and is used only to show or sync scheduled tasks with calendars configured on your device.

Photo library access is optional and is used only when you choose a profile image.

Siri shortcuts are used only to add shopping items when you request it.
